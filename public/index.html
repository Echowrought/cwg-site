Get started

Play in three steps

  1. I

    Download the map

    Grab the latest CWG RPG map file and put it in your Warcraft III maps folder.

    Download the map
  2. II

    Install the art pack

    The CWG Art Installer turns on "Allow Local Files" and installs the model pack, so every hero and boss shows up properly.

    Get the Art Installer

    Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Join a lobby

    Host or join a CWG RPG lobby on Battle.net. Find a party and patch notes in the Discord.
